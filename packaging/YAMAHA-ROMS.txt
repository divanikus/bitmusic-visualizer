Optional YM2608 rhythm ROM

Filename: ym2608_adpcm_rom.bin
Size: exactly 8192 bytes

Windows: roms/ymfm/ beside bitmusic_visualizer.exe.
Linux: $XDG_DATA_HOME/BitMusicVisualizer/roms/ymfm/
       (normally ~/.local/share/BitMusicVisualizer/roms/ymfm/).
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/BitMusicVisualizer/roms/ymfm/.

This ROM is needed only when a track uses YM2608 rhythm samples. FM, SSG and
VGM-embedded ADPCM samples do not require it. The player shows the exact path
when needed. Add the ROM and reopen the music file.

Provide your own ROM. No ROMs are included or downloaded by the application.
YM2610/2610B and Y8950 use sample data contained in the VGM file.
