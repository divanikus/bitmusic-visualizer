Optional external sound files
=============================

Commodore 64: place your own ROM images in roms/c64/ with these exact names:
  kernal.rom   8192 bytes
  basic.rom    8192 bytes
  chargen.rom  4096 bytes

The roms directory is located:
  Windows: beside bitmusic_visualizer.exe
  Linux:   $XDG_DATA_HOME/BitMusicVisualizer/roms
           (default ~/.local/share/BitMusicVisualizer/roms)
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/BitMusicVisualizer/roms

Ordinary PSID and self-contained RSID tunes work without these files. A warning
appears only when the loaded tune needs a missing/unreadable/wrong-size image.
It gives the exact filename and directory. Add the image, then reopen the tune.
The BASIC flag requires basic.rom; other requirements are detected on use.
No ROM files are bundled or downloaded. Size checks do not prove authenticity.
Images are read only; changing them does not affect an already loaded tune.

MIDI / MT-32 and SoundFont playback are not implemented yet.
