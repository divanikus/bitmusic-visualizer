# Bit Music Visualizer 0.6.1

Choose a target of **30, 60 or 120 FPS** in **pencil → Waves… → Frame rate**.
Your choice is remembered across restarts; the default is 30 FPS.

- Wave trails keep their selected duration at higher frame rates.
