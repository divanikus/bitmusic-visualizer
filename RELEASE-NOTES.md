# Bit Music Visualizer 0.9.1

A small hotfix to give MIDI music more room on screen.

- **Fewer empty cards:** General MIDI channels and MT-32 parts with no notes in the file now start hidden. Parts that enter later stay visible.
- Bring any card back with its checkbox in the layout editor. Audio playback is unchanged.
