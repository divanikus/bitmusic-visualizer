# Bit Music Visualizer 0.7.2

## What's new

- **Game Boy GBS:** Square 1/2 and Wave keyboards now show notes and frequencies from chip data.
- **AY:** channels A/B/C now use chip notes, including Spectrum and Amstrad CPC timing.

Click the **pencil**, select a card and choose **View → Keyboard**.
Noise, beeper, FM and Full mix still use experimental estimates.
