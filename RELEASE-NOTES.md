# Bit Music Visualizer 0.9.0

Bit Music Visualizer takes a big step beyond consoles. Put on a C64 tune or a classic MT-32 soundtrack, pick out a part with Solo, and give every waveform the whole screen.

- **More music to explore:** C64 SID, General MIDI and Roland MT-32 join the player, alongside Atari POKEY and more Yamaha FM/sample chips in VGM/VGZ.
- **Hear what each part is doing:** new systems get individual voice cards, MIDI instrument names and Solo with Shift-click or Ctrl+Shift+1–9.
- **Give the music some space:** go fullscreen with F11 or a double-click, tune line thickness in Waves, and switch on L/R level meters beside Stereo.

General MIDI needs your **SF2 SoundFont**; MT-32 needs your **Control and PCM ROMs**. These files are not included. Select MT-32 manually for MIDI files without identifying SysEx.
