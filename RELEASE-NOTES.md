# Bit Music Visualizer 0.11.0

More control over every card, plus new ways to play your MIDI collection.

- **Card settings in one place.** Use a card's pencil to change its view, options and colors.
- **Level meters per card.** Choose where to show them; the toolbar still switches all cards together.
- **Keyboard control for any card.** Select with a click or arrows, then change views, mute/Solo, hide or edit. Alt + arrows move cards, Shift + arrows resize them, and F9/F10 adjust the grid. Press F1 for the full guide.
- **Experimental MIDI format 2 support.** Musical patterns appear as separate playlist tracks, with note-free setup tracks applied in the background.
- **Experimental SFLIST support.** Open existing SF2 sets, including supported foo_midi-style JSON and nested lists, through SoundFont → Browse. Unchanged banks load faster on subsequent tracks.
