# Bit Music Visualizer 0.9.0

- **New music systems:** Commodore 64 SID, General MIDI and Roland MT-32, plus Atari POKEY and more Yamaha FM/sample chips in VGM/VGZ.
- **Channel control:** individual voice cards for the new systems, MIDI instrument names and Solo via Shift-click or Ctrl+Shift+1–9.
- **Visualizer:** fullscreen via F11 or double-click, adjustable line width in Waves, and optional L/R level meters beside Stereo.

General MIDI needs your **SF2 SoundFont**; MT-32 needs your **Control and PCM ROMs**. These files are not included. Select MT-32 manually for MIDI files without identifying SysEx.
