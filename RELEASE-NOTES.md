# Bit Music Visualizer 0.9.2

Hear a part on its own, right from its visualization.

- **Shift+click a card to Solo its channel** — waveform, spectrum or keyboard, including fullscreen.
- Shift-click another channel to switch Solo, or the same channel again to restore your previous mutes.
