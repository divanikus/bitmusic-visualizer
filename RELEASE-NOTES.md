# Bit Music Visualizer 0.10.0

From Amiga modules to falling MIDI notes — more ways to see the music you love.

- **Tracker music is here:** MOD, MED/OctaMED and DigiBooster, plus XM, S3M and IT, with separate channel cards and sample names.
- **Follow the score:** scrolling Pattern cards for trackers and a Piano roll for MIDI. Mix them with waves and spectra in your own layout.
- **Build a listening queue:** M3U playlists with drag reordering, subsongs, shuffle and playlist repeat.
- **Two voices, plenty of character:** Atari 2600 TIATracker (`.ttt`) playback, with no extra ROMs.
- **A tidier workspace:** a tabbed card editor and Settings for playback, rendering and sound resources.
